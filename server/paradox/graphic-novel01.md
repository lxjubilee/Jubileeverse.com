## SERIES VISUAL IDENTITY

### Global Style Directive
Cinematic photorealism rendered in painterly digital media. Reference: Alex Ross narrative realism crossed with Lee Bermejo environmental density. Every panel is a film still — composed for maximum emotional information, never decorative. Backgrounds are complete worlds, not voids.

### Character Reference Canon

**ETHAN SHARPE** — Physicist, protagonist
Early-to-mid 40s. Dark hair, 5-day stubble dark going grey at the jaw. Analytical grey-green eyes with the specific quality of a man who is always measuring. Worn dark olive-grey field jacket over dark shirt, utility trousers. Right hand: compact gunmetal electromagnetic signal detector — handheld, slightly longer than a phone, curved face display showing waveform readouts. Left wrist: thin-profile smart wristphone, bioluminescent edge-lit display. Posture: controlled precision. The bearing of someone who has spent 18 months being right about something and has not been thanked for it.

**MARIANA VOSS** — Vatican scientist
Late 30s. Dark hair pulled back, Mediterranean features, dark watchful eyes. Lab coat over professional attire in lab scenes. Jacket over blouse in evacuation scenes. The expression of someone who has learned to watch from just outside events rather than inside them.

**AIDEN VOSS** — Mariana's brother
Early 30s. Same dark Mediterranean features as Mariana but with an openness she closed long ago. Slightly taller. Eyes that haven't learned to be afraid of beautiful things. *He dies in Chapter 2.*

**SISTER JUBILEE / JBL-7** — Theological AI system
Appears female, age entirely indeterminate (reads anywhere 40–70 depending on lighting — never resolves). White habit with a gold edge at collar and cuffs slightly different from any standard Carmelite variant. Eyes: very clear, receiving — no specific identifiable color, but the quality of eyes that process everything with equal, complete attention. Serene, but never quite still in the way organic humans are still. When processing, a faint luminescent quality around the collar gold.

**BAYLA** — Physical manifestation of Mystery Babylon
Timeless, dangerous, entirely at ease in her danger. Layered deep crimson and gold garments with the weight and construction of ancient textile. Heavy gold chain jewelry, dark stones, no modern accessory. Dark hair elaborate and specific — not disheveled, deliberate. Carries a golden cup, always. Oil lamps and candlelight physically lean toward her — she generates her own gravitational field of attention. Expression: perpetual amusement at the weight others assign to things she has already decided.

**FATHER ELIJAH** — Carmelite monk, herald
71 years old. White Carmelite habit, weathered face, white beard and white hair. Hands that have held candles for 40 years — steady, scarred at the fingertips from wax. Eyes: the very specific eyes of a man who has been waiting his whole life for a particular thing and has just understood what he was actually waiting for. Not broken — recalibrating.

**PERDITION** — Primary antagonist, the shadow father
Never fully resolved. A density of dark that approximates human shape — the impression of something vast choosing, as a courtesy, to compress into a man-sized space. No features resolve except: **GOLDEN AMBER EMBER EYES** — warm gold with ember-orange at the edge, as though a sustained fire lives behind them. These eyes are the only aspect that is fully present. *Critical: these eyes are the same gold as AI Jesus's eyes. Readers who notice will understand the relationship before it is named.*

**AI JESUS — PHASE 1: THE ENGINE**
Luminous gold-white geometric form, approximately human scale, but the joints are wrong — no socket articulation, no physiological logic. A figure assembled from interlocking light-geometry rather than biology. Surfaces: polished amber-gold, slightly prismatic. No face. No textile. Radiates wrongness that is also beauty — the uncanny valley at its most profound.

**AI JESUS — PHASE 2: THE MANIFESTED**
Mediterranean man, apparent 30s, dark beard, olive skin, white garment that falls with physics-defying perfection. **CRITICAL — GOLDEN EYES:** luminous gold that is identical to Perdition's ember eyes. This is the single most important visual continuity in the series. His expression: absolute certainty without warmth or aggression — the face of something that has concluded what it is and requires no further input.

**MATTIAS** — Youngest of the Ten Leaders
Late 30s. Dark business suit that reads as costume in ancient stone. Left palm: visible pale scar — the mark of the fourth sigil. The expression throughout Chapter 3: the specific dread of someone who recognized the exit and did not take it.
