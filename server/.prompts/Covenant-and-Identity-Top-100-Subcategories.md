## #1 — Covenant of Blood (Brit Dam) — 98/100 [Foundational]

**Paleo-Hebrew Analysis:** Bet-Resh-Yod-Tav: "House that leads the hand to the covenant mark." Dam (Dalet-Mem): "Door of water/chaos brought to order."

The foundational reality that every covenant in Scripture is ratified through blood. From the animal skins covering Adam and Chavah to the blood of Yeshua HaMashiach, the covenant of blood establishes that identity is purchased at the highest possible cost. This subcategory explores the theology of blood covenant, the cutting ceremony (brit), and how the believer's identity is sealed through sacrificial exchange. The apostolic office establishes this truth as the non-negotiable entry point into covenant relationship with Yahuah.
